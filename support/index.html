Permissions and folders

Which permissions does Glance ask for?

Only these, and each only when you first use the feature:

  1. Calendars and Reminders for your day in the panel and the Island’s Calendar and Tasks.
  2. Microphone and Speech Recognition while you record Meeting Notes, and for the microphone test on the Sound page.
  3. Screen & System Audio Recording only if you record a meeting with “Hear the other people too” on, to capture the call’s sound.
  4. Your home folder, chosen once in an Open panel, before Storage scans it, and your .claude folder if you want the Island’s Agents tab.
  5. Automation for Music and Spotify, the first time you open the Island’s music card or turn on Now Playing.
  6. Camera only for Mirror in the Island’s tools, while Mirror is on screen.

Glance never asks for Accessibility or your password, and the only apps it controls are Music and Spotify, if you allow it.

I said no to Calendars. How do I change my mind?

Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Calendars and switch Glance on. The same goes for Reminders, Microphone, Speech Recognition, Camera and Screen & System Audio Recording, each under its own name, and for Music and Spotify under Automation. If Glance doesn’t pick up the change straight away, quit it and open it again.

Why does macOS ask whether Glance may control Music or Spotify?

That’s the Automation prompt. Glance asks Music and Spotify, and no other app, for what’s playing (the artwork and where the track is) and to play, pause, skip, scrub, shuffle and repeat from the Island and the floating player. macOS asks once for each app, the first time you open the music card or turn on Now Playing.

If you’d rather not, choose Don’t Allow: the card still shows the track, and a line under it opens the setting if you change your mind. To undo either answer later, open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Automation, find Glance and switch Music or Spotify on or off.

Which folders should I choose, and why?

Mac App Store apps can only look inside folders you choose, so Glance asks in an Open panel and remembers each choice on your Mac:

  1. Your home folder (Choose Home Folder…, on Storage, or under the Island’s screenshot alert): for Storage, new screenshots, files downloading, and synced lyrics in the floating player.
  2. Your .claude folder (Settings ▸ Island ▸ Agents tab ▸ Choose Your .claude Folder…): for your Claude Code sessions in the Island’s Agents tab. It’s hidden in your home folder; in the Open panel, press Command-Shift-. (full stop) to show hidden folders.

Files you drop on the shelf need no folder: Glance remembers each one so it’s still there after a restart. Storage’s clean-ups only ever move items to the Bin, so you can put anything back.

Using Glance

How do I start and stop Stay Awake?

In the panel, choose Stay Awake and how long: an hour, for as long as you choose, or until a download finishes. Choose it again to end it. You can keep the display on or let it sleep, show the time left in the menu bar, and have it stop by itself when the battery runs low.

Can my Mac stay awake with the lid closed?

Yes, with the optional Ashforde Enhancer, a separate free app from Ashforde that isn’t in the Mac App Store. Mac App Store apps aren’t allowed to do this on their own, and Glance works fully without it.

  1. Download Ashforde Enhancer (straight from Ashforde), open it and follow its set-up.
  2. Glance then offers Stay Awake with the Lid Closed. Until the Enhancer is set up, that option doesn’t appear.
Meeting Notes only hears me, not the others.

To hear the other people on a call, Meeting Notes listens to your Mac’s sound, which needs Screen & System Audio Recording. Allow it in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. Without it, only your microphone is written down. Please make sure everyone on the call is happy to be recorded.

Can Glance open when I log in?

Yes. In Glance’s Settings, turn on Open Glance when I log in.

Why is Glance’s menu bar icon dimmed while Monorail is open?

Monorail, Glance’s sibling for your windows, does everything Glance does. When both are open, Glance steps aside so nothing runs twice: Monorail handles Stay Awake and the panel, and Glance’s icon stays in the menu bar, dimmed.

Click the dimmed icon to read why, with Open Monorail and Use Glance Instead. When Monorail quits, Glance takes over again within a few seconds. To hide Glance’s icon while Monorail is open, turn on Hide Glance’s icon while Monorail is open in Settings ▸ General ▸ With Monorail.

Updates, starting afresh and which Macs

How do updates arrive?

Through the Mac App Store, like any App Store app. Glance never checks for or installs updates itself. To get them automatically, open the App Store, choose App Store ▸ Settings and turn on Automatic Updates; otherwise look under Updates in the App Store.

How do I reset Glance and start afresh?
  1. Quit Glance from its menu.
  2. In Finder, choose Go ▸ Go to Folder…, type ~/Library/Containers and press Return.
  3. Move the folder named Glance to the Bin. It holds Glance’s settings, notes and recordings, so copy anything you want to keep first.
  4. Open Glance again. It starts as if new, with the welcome tour.

To reset a permission instead, switch Glance off and on again in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. To remove Glance completely, delete it from Applications as well.

Which Macs does Glance run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later. A few things depend on your Mac: battery readings need a Mac with a battery, and summaries in Meeting Notes need macOS 26.

Contact

How do I report a problem or suggest an idea?

Write to contact@ashforde.org. Tell us your Mac model, your macOS version, Glance’s version (in its About page) and what happened. A screenshot helps. We read everything and reply by email.

What does Glance send about me?

Nothing to Ashforde: no account, no analytics, no usage counts. It goes online only to fetch what it shows you. The privacy policy lists every service it contacts, and when.
