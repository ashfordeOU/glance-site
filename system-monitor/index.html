The menu bar panel

Click Glance’s icon in the menu bar and the panel drops down. At the top, a line says whether all is calm; below it, your day with the next meeting and a Join button, and cards for your system, storage and network. Cards rise to the top when they matter.

Twelve one-click switches sit underneath: Stay Awake, Evening Mode, Microphone, Camera, Meeting Notes, Record Screen and more. Right-click a switch for its options.

Battery health and cycles

The panel shows your battery’s charge, its health and its cycle count, along with the battery of your mouse, keyboard and trackpad. The Island’s Battery tab draws the charge as pixel bars, with the time left, the health, the cycles and how many watts your Mac is using right now. Battery readings need a Mac with a battery.

The Dashboard

The gauge button at the top of the panel opens the Dashboard: one window for everything Glance reads, in eight groups along the top: Overview, Apps, Power, Performance, Storage, Connections, Your day and Insights.

  • Overview answers “Why is my Mac slow?” above tiles for CPU, memory, GPU, network, disk and battery.
  • CPU draws each core laid out as on your chip; Memory and GPU have pages of their own.
  • Network has a speed test that runs only when you start it.
  • AI Usage keeps an eye on your AI plans’ limits.
  • History and Trends show how things have changed over time.

Light on your Mac

Readings stop when you close the panel, and live drawings rest when you can’t see them, so the monitor doesn’t become the thing slowing your Mac down.

Questions

How do I check my MacBook’s battery health?

Click Glance’s icon in the menu bar: the panel shows the battery’s health and cycle count. The Island’s Battery tab and the Dashboard’s Battery page show them too.

Where do I see CPU and memory use?

In the panel’s System card, and at full size on the Dashboard’s CPU, Memory and GPU pages. The Island’s Memory tab shows memory as pixel bars, with the pressure and the apps using the most.

Can I run an internet speed test?

Yes, on the Dashboard’s Network page. It runs only when you start it.

Does Glance need administrator rights?

No. Glance never asks for your password, administrator rights or Accessibility.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later. Battery readings need a Mac with a battery.
