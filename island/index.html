What the Island does

On a MacBook with a camera in the screen, the Island sits around it, in space the menu bar can’t use anyway. On a Mac without one, it’s a small pill at the top centre of the screen. Either way it stays out of the way until something is going on.

Closed: just bubbles

Out of the box the Island uses Just bubbles: it hides behind the camera, and each live thing floats beside it in its own round bubble. What’s playing shows its artwork with moving bars; a timer or Stay Awake shows a ring for the time left; charging shows how full the battery is. Point at a bubble for its own card. Prefer it in one piece? Choose Split bubbles or One pill in Settings ▸ Island.

Open: a tab for each part of your day

Click the camera and the Island grows into a black card with its tab bar floating below it:

  • Home: your switches (Stay Awake, Evening Mode, Microphone, Camera, Meeting Notes, Record Screen and more), a line to jot a note, what’s playing, the weather and your world clocks.
  • Calendar: your agenda, with events added in words, such as “Lunch with Sam on Friday at 1pm”.
  • Timer: named timers typed in plain words, focus sessions and a stopwatch.
  • Tasks: your reminders and a week planner.
  • Insights: your time at the Mac, focus sessions and breaks.
  • Storage, Battery and Memory: pixel bars you can point at, on by default; turn any of them off from the +.
  • Tools: the Song finder, a calculator, a converter, notes, clipboard history, emoji, a teleprompter, Mirror and more.

Music, with lyrics

When Music or Spotify is playing, the Island shows the artwork, synced lyrics and the controls: play, pause, skip, scrub, shuffle and repeat. Detach the card to keep a floating player on screen.

Drop files on it

Drag a file to the top of the screen and the Island opens as a drop target. The file waits on the shelf until you drag it out, AirDrop it or share it as a link.

Made to stay out of the way

Glance never asks for Accessibility or your password, and the Island never moves other apps’ windows. Live drawings rest when you can’t see them, so it stays light on your Mac. Everything about the Island, from where it sits and how it looks to its tools, alerts, colours and live items, has its own page: Settings ▸ Island.

Questions

Is it like the Dynamic Island?

Yes: the Island sits around your MacBook’s camera notch, like the Dynamic Island on iPhone, and shows what’s going on in small bubbles beside the camera. Click the camera to open it, with tabs for Home, your calendar, timers, tasks and tools.

Does the Island work on a Mac without a camera in the screen?

Yes. On a Mac without a camera in the screen, the Island is a small pill at the top centre of the screen, with the same bubbles, tabs and shelf.

Can I change how the closed Island looks?

Yes. Just bubbles is the style for new installs: the Island hides behind the camera and each live thing gets its own bubble. Split bubbles and One pill keep it in one piece. Choose in Settings ▸ Island, where you can also pick its tabs, tools, alerts and colours.

Which music apps does the Island show?

Music and Spotify. Glance asks each of them for what’s playing and to play, pause and skip, after macOS asks you once. If you say no, the track still shows, without the buttons.

Does the Island need Accessibility access?

No. Glance never asks for Accessibility or your password, and it never moves other apps’ windows. The only apps it controls are Music and Spotify, if you allow it.
