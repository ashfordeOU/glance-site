Between docks

Drag an app to another dock.

Drag an app from one dock onto another to move it there. Hold Option as you drag to copy it instead.

Add to Dock, Move to Dock

Right-click to send it elsewhere.

An app’s menu in a dock lists your other docks under Add to Dock, with a tick by those that hold it already, and, for an app you keep there, Move to Dock.

The app you’re using

Add the front app to a dock.

Press the shortcut you set in Settings ▸ Docks, or choose Add … to a Dock in the command palette, and a menu of your docks opens at the pointer. Pick one, or make a new dock with that app.

Add Apps

Search your apps, add several at once.

Add ▸ App… in Settings ▸ Docks lists the apps on your Mac to search and tick. Apps already in the dock are marked, and Other… chooses a folder, a file or an app somewhere else.

Docks of your own

Right-click Glance’s icon in the menu bar and choose Add a Dock…, then put it on any edge of the screen. An Apps dock shows a dot under each open app and icons that grow as you pass; a Widgets bar shows readings such as the time, the battery and the weather; or have both in one. Docks hold apps, folders and files, and sit alongside the Mac’s own Dock.

Moving things between docks

  • Drag an app from one dock onto another to move it there. Hold Option as you drag to copy it instead.
  • Right-click an app: Add to Dock lists your other docks, with a tick by those that hold it already, and Move to Dock sends an app you keep there somewhere else.
  • Press the shortcut you set in Settings ▸ Docks and a menu of your docks opens at the pointer, to add the app you’re using. Pick a dock, or make a new one with that app.
  • Add ▸ App… in Settings ▸ Docks lists the apps on your Mac to search and tick, several at once; Other… adds a folder, a file or an app from somewhere else.

Widgets anywhere

Put a widget on the Island’s Home, the desktop, the Dashboard, a dock or the menu bar panel. Pods are built-in widgets for headlines, crypto prices, GitHub notifications and a word of the day; each goes online only once you turn it on.

Out of your way

Glance’s docks don’t need Accessibility and never move other apps’ windows. They’re there when you want an app, a folder or a reading, and nothing else changes.

Questions

Can I have more than one dock on my Mac?

Yes. Add docks on any edge of the screen, each with its own apps, folders, files and widgets, and drag apps between them.

Does Glance replace the Mac’s own Dock?

No. Glance’s docks are docks of your own that sit alongside the Mac’s Dock, which stays as it is.

Where can I put widgets?

On the Island’s Home, the desktop, the Dashboard, a dock or the menu bar panel.

What are Pods?

Built-in widgets for headlines, crypto prices, GitHub notifications and a word of the day. Each goes online only once you turn it on.

How do I move an app from one dock to another?

Drag it onto the other dock, or right-click it and choose Move to Dock. Hold Option as you drag to copy it instead.
