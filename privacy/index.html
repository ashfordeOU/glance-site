Privacy

Your Mac’s business stays on your Mac.

Glance has no account and collects nothing. Ashforde receives nothing from it. Here is exactly what goes online, where, and when.

Effective 15 November 2026Data controller Ashforde OÜApplies to Glance for Mac (on the App Store as “Glance: Island, Widgets, Stats”)

Never leaves your Mac

  • Battery, health and charge cycles, and your accessories’ batteries
  • Processor, memory, storage and network readings, and their history
  • Your calendar events and reminders
  • Meeting Notes recordings, and transcripts and summaries made on your Mac
  • Your clipboard history, notes and the files on the Island’s shelf
  • What Storage finds in your home folder
  • The bookmarks for the folders you choose (your home folder and your .claude folder) and for the files on the shelf, and what Glance reads in them
  • What’s playing in Music or Spotify
  • Mirror’s camera picture and Privacy Guard’s list of blocked attempts

Goes online, straight to the service

  • Weather for the places you add, from Open-Meteo, and the rain radar while you look at it
  • Exchange rates, when you convert a currency
  • The current track’s artwork from Spotify’s image server, while Spotify is playing
  • A speed test, only when you start one
  • Your public IP lookup on the Network page (you can turn it off)
  • AI services you connect yourself, with your own key

What Glance collects

Nothing about you, unless you choose to send it. Glance needs no account. It sends no analytics and no usage counts, doesn’t check for updates itself (the Mac App Store does that), shows no ads, has no tracking and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. Two things reach Ashforde only when you choose them: files you share with a share link, and a crash report you decide to email us.

Glance doesn’t ask for Accessibility and never asks for your password or for administrator rights. The only apps it sends instructions to are Music and Spotify, and only if you allow it (see Automation below).

Outside services Glance contacts

Each of these is contacted directly from your Mac, only for the reason given, and never through Ashforde. Like any website, each service sees the IP address the request comes from. None of them receives an account, a name or anything that links one request to you, and Ashforde receives none of it.

Share links

When you choose Share Link on a file, Glance uploads that file to Ashforde’s share service, which runs on Cloudflare, and copies a link anyone can open. The file is kept for the time you pick (1, 7 or 30 days) and then deleted; you can delete it sooner from Glance. Comments people leave on a shared video are kept with the share and deleted with it. The service stores no IP addresses and no account. If you’d rather keep files in your own storage, choose your Dropbox in Settings and the link points there instead. Anyone can report a share from its page; a report keeps what the person types (the reason, any details and an email address if they give one) and the shared files’ names, for up to 31 days, with no IP address. See the terms for share links.

Crash reports

Glance reads crash details from macOS (MetricKit), on your Mac. If Glance quits unexpectedly, it may offer once to send the crash report: Send opens an email to contact@ashforde.org in your own mail app with the Glance and macOS versions, your Mac’s model and where it crashed, and puts the full report on the clipboard for you to paste in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. To stop the offer, switch off Offer to send crash reports in Glance’s Settings, under General ▸ Privacy.

Weather is credited as “Weather by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)”. Links in Glance open in your own browser; Glance has no web view of its own.

Permissions, and what stays on your Mac

Every permission is optional and asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change any of them in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security.

  • Calendars and Reminders: for your day in the panel, meeting reminders, and the Island’s Calendar and Tasks. Events and reminders are read and written on your Mac and never sent anywhere by Glance.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only while you record Meeting Notes, and for the microphone level test on the Sound page. Recordings stay on your Mac. Transcripts are made on your Mac where macOS supports it; where it doesn’t, macOS may use Apple’s speech recognition service, under Apple’s privacy policy. On macOS 26, summaries come from Apple’s on-device model.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording: only if you record a meeting with Hear the other people too switched on, to capture the call’s sound. Nothing is shared. Switch that setting off and macOS never asks. Please make sure everyone on a call is happy to be recorded.
  • Automation (Music and Spotify): the first time you open the Island’s music card or turn on Now Playing, macOS asks whether Glance may control Music, and separately Spotify. Glance uses this only to read what’s playing (the track, its artwork and where it is) and to play, pause, skip, scrub, shuffle and repeat. It asks no other app. If you say no, the card still shows the track, without the buttons. You can change it any time in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Automation.
  • Files and folders: Mac App Store apps can only look inside folders you choose, so Glance asks in an Open panel and remembers each choice with a bookmark that stays on your Mac:
    • Your home folder, chosen once for Storage. What it finds stays on your Mac, and clean-ups only ever move items to the Bin. AI Usage reads AI tools’ usage logs from it, and the Island uses it to show new screenshots, files downloading, and synced lyrics from Music/Lyrics.
    • Your .claude folder, only if you choose it for the Island’s Agents tab. Glance reads the Claude Code session files in it, only while the tab is on screen, to show each session’s task, state and cost so far. They are never sent anywhere.
    • Files you drop on the shelf: Glance keeps a bookmark for each, so the shelf still has them after a restart. Removing a file from every shelf forgets its bookmark.
  • Camera: only for Mirror in the Island’s tools, and only while Mirror is on screen. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Privacy Guard: when you block the microphone, Glance mutes your Mac’s input devices and sets their input volume to zero, and puts your volumes back when you unblock. It watches whether a microphone or camera is in use and which app is using it, so it can name the app in a warning; it never listens to the microphone or looks through the camera. Glance can’t quit other apps, so for the camera it warns only. The list of the last blocked attempts stays on your Mac.

The clipboard history never keeps passwords or other concealed copies. Weather needs no location access at all.

Ashforde Enhancer (optional)

Ashforde Enhancer is a separate, free app that Ashforde distributes outside the Mac App Store, from monorail.ashforde.org. Glance works fully without it, and Glance never downloads or installs it. If you install the Enhancer and set it up, it places a script in Glance’s own Application Scripts folder, and Glance then offers Stay Awake with the Lid Closed, running that script on your Mac. Nothing about you is sent to Ashforde either way.

Children

Glance is rated 4+. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

This website

glance.ashforde.org sets no cookies and runs no analytics or counters. If you choose Light or Dark with the appearance button, that choice is kept in your browser’s local storage on this device only and never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages: to deliver the pages, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your rights

Because Glance collects nothing, Ashforde holds no personal data about you from it. Under the GDPR you may still ask us what we hold, and if you write to us, we use your email only to reply and keep it no longer than needed for that. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If Glance ever starts to send anything new, this page will say so before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
