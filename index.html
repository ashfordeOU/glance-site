The Island

Your Mac, one glance up.

Switches, your next meeting, the weather and world clocks in the Island at the top of your screen.

It hangs from the camera and shows what’s playing. Timers, Stay Awake, charging and an AI limit float beside it in round bubbles, each with a ring for its time or charge. Rest the pointer on it to peek, click to open it, and pick a tab from the bar below.

Music and Spotify

The artwork, where the track is, and play, pause, skip, scrub, shuffle and repeat. macOS asks once before Glance may control each app.

A shelf that works with your files

Drop files on the Island to keep them handy. Unzip archives, fit a picture or a video under a size you type, remove a picture’s background, and let folders you choose fill the shelf by themselves.

Storage, Battery and Memory

Three tabs with pixel bars, on by default. Point at a block to read what it is. Turn any of them off from the +.

World clocks and weather

Your cities at a glance, and the weather for the places you choose, from Apple Weather, with the rain radar, the next hour and the next 48 hours.

Screenshots and downloads

Each new screenshot, ready to keep on the shelf, and files downloading with their progress, once you choose your home folder.

Claude Code in Agents

Choose your .claude folder and the Agents tab lists your sessions from the last few hours: the task, its state and the cost so far.

Mirror

A quick look at yourself before a call. The camera is on only while Mirror shows, and nothing is recorded.

Island settings of its own

Where it sits, its look, its tools, alerts, colours and live items, all on its own page: Settings ▸ Island.

The shelf

Drop files on the Island.

Keep them on the shelf, change them there, then drag them out wherever they’re going.

Right-click one to open it, AirDrop it, rename or pin it, or transform it: unzip an archive, trim a clip, make a GIF, fit a picture or a video under a size you type, or remove a picture’s background.

Stay Awake

For an hour, or until the download’s done.

Keep your Mac awake for 15 minutes to 8 hours, until you end it or while something downloads. The Island shows a ring with the time left, and it can keep the display on and keep you Available in Teams and Slack.

Evening Mode

A warmer screen at night.

Less blue light on every display. Drag Warmth and it comes on; turn it on by hand, every day at set times or from sunset to sunrise, and let it rest while apps where colour matters are in front.

Island tools

Name that song.

Song finder and a drawer of tools in the Island, from a calculator to a teleprompter.

The Song finder listens for up to 12 seconds, to the room or to your Mac’s own sound, and names the song with Shazam, with links to open it. It listens only when you press Listen, and keeps the last 50 songs it found. Turn on the tools you want in the Tools tab.

Record and share

Record it. Share a link.

Record your screen or a call, get a transcript, then share a link anyone can open.

Record Screen is on Home and in the Island’s Recordings, with your Mac’s sound, your microphone or both. Choose Share Link on a file on the shelf and Glance copies a link anyone can open in a browser, with no account: up to 25 files, kept for 1, 7 or 30 days. Nothing is uploaded until you choose Share Link.

Docks

Docks and widgets, anywhere.

Docks on any edge with apps, folders and widgets, and Pods for the desktop, the Island and more.

An Apps dock with a dot under each open app and icons that grow as you pass, a Widgets bar of readings, or both. Move apps between docks by dragging or from their menu, and add the app you’re using in a keystroke.

Between docks

Drag an app to another dock.

Drag an app from one dock onto another to move it there. Hold Option as you drag to copy it instead.

Add to Dock, Move to Dock

Right-click to send it elsewhere.

An app’s menu in a dock lists your other docks under Add to Dock, with a tick by those that hold it already, and, for an app you keep there, Move to Dock.

The app you’re using

Add the front app to a dock.

Press the shortcut you set in Settings ▸ Docks, or choose Add … to a Dock in the command palette, and a menu of your docks opens at the pointer. Pick one, or make a new dock with that app.

Add Apps

Search your apps, add several at once.

Add ▸ App… in Settings ▸ Docks lists the apps on your Mac to search and tick. Apps already in the dock are marked, and Other… chooses a folder, a file or an app somewhere else.

Storage

Tidy, private, rested.

Clear space safely, know when an app listens or watches, and take a break now and then.

Choose your home folder once, and Smart clean-up finds caches, old logs, Xcode’s files, packages in projects you haven’t touched lately and old downloads. Point at a colour to see why it’s safe, review it all, and it goes to the Bin, so you can put it back.

The welcome

It starts with hello. Then a short tour.

The first time Glance opens, it writes hello, then takes about two minutes to show you the Island, the panel and your day, with a live look at each. Skip it, or open it again any time from Settings.

And more

The small things, done properly.

Privacy Guard

One click in the panel blocks the microphone for every app. Block the camera too, and Glance warns you the moment an app turns one on. Allow the apps you trust.

Meeting Notes

Record a call and get a transcript, made on your Mac where macOS supports it. On macOS 26, a summary too.

A floating player

Detach the music card and keep it on screen, with its buttons and synced lyrics from your Music folder.

Clipboard history

Find anything you copied and copy it again. Passwords and other concealed copies are never kept.

Text and QR codes

Pull the text or a QR code out of a picture or a screenshot you’ve copied.

Docks of your own

Apps, folders, files and small widgets, at any edge of the screen.

Breaks

Gentle reminders to rest your eyes, stretch and drink some water.

A ringed menu bar icon

Glance’s icon sits inside the ring from its app icon, so it’s easy to tell apart. In Colour, the pulse is Glance’s pink.

Easy on your Mac

Live drawings rest when you can’t see them, and readings stop when you close the panel, so Glance stays light.

A closer look

Every screen, up close.

The Dashboard, Weather, Time, the panel’s pages and the Island’s tabs, drawn as they look on your Mac.

Private by design

It keeps to itself. And to your Mac.

Everything Glance reads stays on your Mac. It goes online only to fetch what it shows you.
