The menu bar panel

Your Mac, one click away.

Click Glance’s icon in the menu bar and the panel drops down: your day, how your Mac is doing, and twelve one-click switches, from Stay Awake and Evening Mode to Microphone, Camera and Meeting Notes. Right-click a switch for its options.

Open a page

  • Battery, health and cycles
  • Your day and reminders
  • Processor, memory, storage, network
  • Cards that rise when they matter
The Island

What’s happening now, at the top of your screen.

The Island hangs from the camera and shows what’s playing. Timers, Stay Awake, charging and an AI limit float beside it in round bubbles, each with a ring for its time or charge. Rest the pointer on it to peek, click to open it, and pick a tab from the bar below.

Closed

Open a tab

Music and Spotify

The artwork, where the track is, and play, pause, skip, scrub, shuffle and repeat. macOS asks once before Glance may control each app.

A shelf that keeps files

Drop files on the Island to keep them handy. They’re still there the next time Glance opens, on every shelf.

Storage, Battery and Memory

Three tabs with pixel bars, on by default. Point at a block to read what it is. Turn any of them off from the +.

World clocks and weather

Your cities at a glance, and the weather for the places you choose, with the rain radar and the next 48 hours.

Screenshots and downloads

Each new screenshot, ready to keep on the shelf, and files downloading with their progress, once you choose your home folder.

Claude Code in Agents

Choose your .claude folder and the Agents tab lists your sessions from the last few hours: the task, its state and the cost so far.

Mirror

A quick look at yourself before a call. The camera is on only while Mirror shows, and nothing is recorded.

Island settings of its own

Where it sits, its look, its tools, alerts, colours and live items, all in Settings ▸ General ▸ Dynamic Island.

Docks · new in 1.9.9

Docks of your own. On any edge.

Add an Apps dock with a dot under each open app and icons that grow as you pass, a Widgets bar of readings, or both, on any edge of the screen. Right-click a dock to hide it.

The welcome · new in 1.9.9

It starts with hello. Then a short tour.

The first time Glance opens, it writes hello, then takes about two minutes to show you the Island, the panel and your day, with a live look at each. Skip it, or open it again any time from Settings.

And more

The small things, done properly.

Privacy Guard

One click in the panel blocks the microphone for every app. Block the camera too, and Glance warns you the moment an app turns one on. Allow the apps you trust.

Meeting Notes

Record a call and get a transcript, made on your Mac where macOS supports it. On macOS 26, a summary too.

A floating player

Detach the music card and keep it on screen, with its buttons and synced lyrics from your Music folder.

Clipboard history

Find anything you copied and copy it again. Passwords and other concealed copies are never kept.

Text and QR codes

Pull the text or a QR code out of a picture or a screenshot you’ve copied.

Docks of your own

Apps, folders, files and small widgets, at any edge of the screen.

Breaks

Gentle reminders to rest your eyes, stretch and drink some water.

A ringed menu bar icon

Glance’s icon now sits inside the ring from its app icon, so it’s easy to tell apart. In Colour, the pulse is Glance’s pink.

Easy on your Mac

Live drawings rest when you can’t see them, and readings stop when you close the panel, so Glance stays light.

A closer look

Every screen, up close.

The Dashboard, Weather, Time, the panel’s pages and the Island’s tabs, drawn as they look on your Mac.

Private by design

It keeps to itself. And to your Mac.

Everything Glance reads stays on your Mac. It goes online only to fetch what it shows you.
