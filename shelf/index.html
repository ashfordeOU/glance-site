How the shelf works

Drag a file towards the top of the screen and the Island opens with a drop area. Let go, and the file joins the shelf. It stays there, even after a restart, until you clear it or drag it out into an email, a chat, a folder or another app.

Glance remembers each file where it is, so the shelf needs no folder access. With the Island’s tabs hidden, a single shelf button with the number of files takes their place, so the shelf is always one click away.

Change a file without opening an app

Right-click a file on the shelf to open it, AirDrop it, rename it or pin it. Transform has the everyday jobs that usually mean opening three different apps:

  • Convert and resize pictures, or fit a picture under a size you type.
  • Remove a picture’s background.
  • Trim a clip, make a smaller video, a short GIF, or an M4A from its sound.
  • Compress or merge PDFs.
  • Zip files, or unzip an archive.

Share from the shelf

Choose Share Link and Glance uploads the files you picked and copies a link anyone can open in a browser, with no account: up to 25 files, kept for 1, 7 or 30 days, and deleted whenever you like. Nothing is uploaded until you choose Share Link. You can share to your own Dropbox instead. AirDrop sends one file, or everything on the shelf, to a Mac or iPhone nearby.

Screenshots and downloads

Once you choose your home folder, each new screenshot shows in the Island, ready to keep on the shelf, and files downloading show their progress there too.

Questions

Does the shelf need access to my folders?

No. Files you drop on the shelf need no folder: Glance remembers each one, so it’s still there after a restart. Only screenshots and downloads need your home folder, chosen once.

Can I AirDrop files from the shelf?

Yes. Right-click a file and choose AirDrop, or use AirDrop at the top of the shelf to send everything on it.

How long does a share link last?

1, 7 or 30 days, as you choose. Up to 25 files fit in one link, and anyone with it can open them in a browser, with no account. You can delete a share sooner in Settings ▸ Island ▸ Sharing and accounts.

What can Transform do to a file?

Convert and resize pictures, fit a picture or a video under a size you type, remove a picture’s background, trim a clip, make a GIF or a smaller video, take the sound out as M4A, compress or merge PDFs, and zip or unzip.
