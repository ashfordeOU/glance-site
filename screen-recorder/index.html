Record Screen

Start Record Screen from the Island’s Home, its Recordings list or the menu bar panel’s switch. Choose what to record: the whole screen, one display, a window or an area. Then choose the sound: your Mac’s own sound, your microphone, both or neither. Pause and stop from the Island, where the time counts up as you record.

Your Mac’s sound is recorded through macOS’s own Screen & System Audio Recording, so there’s no audio driver to install. macOS asks once for that permission, and for the microphone when you include it.

Meeting Notes

Meeting Notes records a call and writes the transcript. Where macOS supports it, the transcript is made on your Mac, and on macOS 26 you also get a summary. With Hear the other people too on, it listens to your Mac’s sound as well as your microphone, so everyone on the call is written down, not just you. Please make sure everyone is happy to be recorded.

Share a link

Choose Share Link on a recording and Glance copies a link anyone can open in a browser, with no account. Links last 1, 7 or 30 days and can be deleted at any time; nothing is uploaded until you choose Share Link. A video shared for review can take comments from anyone with the link, each at its moment in the video. You can share to your own Dropbox instead.

Kept on your Mac

Recordings and transcripts stay on your Mac, in the Island’s Recordings, until you choose to share one. Glance has no account and nothing to sign in to.

Questions

Can I record my Mac’s screen with system audio?

Yes. Choose your Mac’s sound, your microphone or both before you start. Glance uses macOS’s Screen & System Audio Recording permission, so you don’t need to install an audio driver.

Can I record just one window or part of the screen?

Yes: the whole screen, one display, a window or an area.

Where is the Meeting Notes transcript made?

On your Mac where macOS supports it; otherwise macOS uses Apple’s speech service. Summaries need macOS 26.

Meeting Notes only hears me. Why?

To hear the other people on a call, Meeting Notes listens to your Mac’s sound, which needs Screen & System Audio Recording. Allow it in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. Without it, only your microphone is written down.

How do I share a recording?

Choose Share Link on it. Glance copies a link anyone can open in a browser, kept for 1, 7 or 30 days.
