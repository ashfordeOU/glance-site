Storage

Tidy, private, rested.

Clear space safely, know when an app listens or watches, and take a break now and then.

Choose your home folder once, and Smart clean-up finds caches, old logs, Xcode’s files, packages in projects you haven’t touched lately and old downloads. Point at a colour to see why it’s safe, review it all, and it goes to the Bin, so you can put it back. The Microphone switch mutes every microphone and says which app tried to listen; the Camera switch tells you when an app turns a camera on. Breaks remind you to rest your eyes, stretch and drink some water.

See what’s taking up space

Mac App Store apps can only look inside folders you choose, so Storage asks once for your home folder in an Open panel and remembers it. The Island’s Storage tab then draws your disk as pixel bars, from Applications and Documents to Photos, Music and System Data. Point at a block to see what it is. The Dashboard’s Storage and Disk pages show more.

Smart clean-up

Smart clean-up looks for what’s safe to clear:

  • App caches.
  • Old logs.
  • Xcode’s build files.
  • Packages in projects you haven’t touched lately.
  • Old downloads.

Each kind gets a colour on one bar, with its size and the total you could get back. Point at a colour to read why it’s safe to clear, then Review and Clean… lets you tick exactly what goes.

Nothing is deleted outright

Clean-ups only ever move items to the Bin, so you can put anything back until you empty it. Glance never asks for your password or administrator rights.

Also looking after you

Glance has two privacy switches too. The Microphone switch mutes every microphone and says which app tried to listen; the Camera switch tells you when an app turns a camera on. Breaks remind you to rest your eyes, stretch and drink some water.

Questions

Is it safe to clear caches on a Mac?

Caches are copies apps can make again, so clearing them is generally safe; an app may be a little slower the first time it rebuilds them. Smart clean-up says why each kind is safe, and everything goes to the Bin, so you can put it back.

Why does Glance ask me to choose my home folder?

Mac App Store apps can only look inside folders you choose. Choose your home folder once and Glance remembers it.

Does Glance delete files permanently?

No. Clean-ups move items to the Bin. They’re gone only when you empty it.

Does it need Full Disk Access?

No. Glance looks only inside the home folder you choose, and never asks for your password or administrator rights.
